# Local validation against Punjab EPA reference stations

Spot check: our Lahore city value (median of OpenAQ sensors) against the mean of Punjab EPA's
government monitoring stations for the same hour (aqi.punjab.gov.pk). Generated by
`python -m smog validate-local` from `config/punjab_epa_reference.csv`.

**4 hours compared.** On average our value was 10.2 µg/m³ lower than the EPA station mean (average ratio 0.89); it fell inside the range of the EPA stations in 4 of 4 hours.

| time_pkt | epa_stations | epa_mean | epa_range | ours_same_hour | our_sensors | ours_24h_mean | difference | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-09 19:00 | 10 | 92.3 | 64.9-135.6 | 79.5 | 45 | 78.4 | -12.8 | 0.86 |
| 2026-10-19 03:00 | 11 | 160.2 | 98.7-238.2 | 166.0 | 46 | 101.6 | 5.8 | 1.04 |
| 2026-10-20 05:00 | 11 | 146.6 | 94-228.4 | 137.0 | 47 | 102.0 | -9.6 | 0.93 |
| 2026-10-20 13:00 | 5 | 82.7 | 42.4-124.4 | 58.5 | 42 | 98.3 | -24.2 | 0.71 |

Limits: only a few hours; the EPA readings were copied from news reports of the EPA dashboard (links in
the CSV); the page does not state its averaging period, so the 24-hour column is shown too; EPA stations
and our sensors are at different places in the city. A systematic difference here would be a reason to
correct the low-cost sensors before using them for official decisions.
