## PM2.5 guideline levels
In 2021 the World Health Organization published new global air quality guidelines. For fine particles (PM2.5) the
recommended 24-hour guideline level is 15 µg/m³ and the annual guideline level is 5 µg/m³.

## PM2.5 interim targets
For countries that cannot yet meet the guideline, WHO sets stepping-stone interim targets. For the 24-hour PM2.5
average the interim targets are 75, 50, 37.5 and 25 µg/m³. For the annual PM2.5 average they are 35, 25, 15 and 10 µg/m³.

## How the 24-hour value is meant
The 24-hour guideline levels are defined as the 99th percentile of daily values, which means about 3 to 4 days per
year above the level are tolerated.

## PM10 guideline levels
For coarser particles (PM10) the 24-hour guideline level is 45 µg/m³ and the annual guideline level is 15 µg/m³.
