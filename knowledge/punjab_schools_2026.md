## The instructions
On 18 August 2026 the Punjab School Education Department issued instructions under the Pre-Smog Action Plan 2026
(July-September 2026), prepared under the Punjab Environmental Protection Agency. Schools must limit outdoor
activities, check the Air Quality Index before allowing outdoor exercise, require students to wear face masks during
school hours, restrict outdoor play when air quality is poor, avoid visits to areas with unhealthy AQI or during
severe AQI episodes, and keep doors and windows closed. No numeric AQI threshold is given.
