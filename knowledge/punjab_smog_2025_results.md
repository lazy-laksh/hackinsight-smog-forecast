## Reported improvement
The Punjab Environment Department reported in December 2025 that Lahore's daily PM2.5 peaks in November 2025 fell
56 percent, to 237 µg/m³, from 539 µg/m³ in November 2024. The monthly average fell 37 percent and year-to-date
pollution fell 15.6 percent.

## Enforcement actions reported
Against polluting industry: 2,575 illegal units demolished, 2,500 premises sealed, 4,000 FIRs registered and
Rs 711 million in fines. Against brick kilns: 485 kilns demolished, 3,178 sealed, 1,171 FIRs and Rs 276 million in
fines. Anti-smog guns sprayed about 55,000 km of urban corridors and heavy vehicles were inspected for emissions.

## Official monitoring network
The EPA Punjab AQI platform (website and app) gives hourly data for six pollutants from 41 stations in 18 districts,
with health advisories and historical data. The release names industrial, vehicular and agricultural emissions,
urban dust and crop-burning smoke as sources.
