## What was reported
Smog appeared in Islamabad, Rawalpindi and major Punjab cities before the official smog season. Reported AQI values
were 101 in Islamabad, 97 in Rawalpindi and about 210 in Lahore.

## Causes and measures named
Causes named: brick kilns without zigzag technology, smoke-emitting vehicles and motorcycles, factories and bakeries,
and burning of garbage, tyres, crop residue and plastic. Measures: from 1 October brick kilns without zigzag
technology are to be demolished or sealed; crackdowns by the environment department, transport authority, civil
defence and district administrations; drone and night-squad monitoring of hotspots; and fines for burning crop
residue and plastic.
