## Updated PM2.5 breakpoints
EPA updated the Air Quality Index (AQI) for PM2.5 in a final rule announced on 7 February 2024; the update took effect
on 6 May 2024. The PM2.5 breakpoints are 24-hour average concentrations:
Good (AQI 0-50) is 0.0 to 9.0 µg/m³. Moderate (AQI 51-100) is 9.1 to 35.4 µg/m³.
Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups (AQI 101-150) is 35.5 to 55.4 µg/m³. Unhealthy (AQI 151-200) is 55.5 to 125.4 µg/m³.
Very Unhealthy (AQI 201-300) is 125.5 to 225.4 µg/m³. Hazardous (AQI 301 and above) is 225.5 µg/m³ and above.

## Why this project uses 55.5
This project calls a day "unhealthy" when the city's daily mean PM2.5 is 55.5 µg/m³ or more, which is the lower
edge of the US EPA "Unhealthy" category above. The 24-hour basis of the breakpoints is confirmed in 40 CFR Part 58,
Appendix G, Table 2 (https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/40/appendix-G_to_part_58).
