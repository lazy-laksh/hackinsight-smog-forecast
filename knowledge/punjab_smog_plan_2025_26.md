## Main measures
The plan covers transport (a digital transport governance system, vehicle inspection, 2,953 e-bikes and electric
buses), agriculture (Super Seeders, rental subsidies and 2,000 modern machinery packages so farmers need not burn
crop residue), environment (50 AQI screens in Lahore, five carbon sinks, plantation drives), energy (solarisation of
tube wells and public buildings) and health (smog centres and anti-smog counters in hospitals).

## Monitoring and forecasting
The plan describes a Smog Monitoring Dashboard that tracks AQI in real time and gives early warnings, using inputs
from 20 departments, satellite data, ground sensors and AI-based forecasting. The plan page does not give numeric
AQI trigger levels for actions.
