## The notification
Pakistan's National Environmental Quality Standards (NEQS) for ambient air were notified as S.R.O. 1062(I)/2010,
dated 18 October 2010 and published in the Gazette of Pakistan (Extraordinary) on 26 November 2010.

## PM2.5 standards
For PM2.5 the standard from 1 January 2013 is 35 µg/m³ as a 24-hour average and 15 µg/m³ as an annual average.
The earlier values, from 1 July 2010, were 40 µg/m³ (24 hours) and 25 µg/m³ (annual).

## Comparison with WHO
Pakistan's 24-hour PM2.5 standard of 35 µg/m³ is higher (less strict) than the WHO 2021 24-hour guideline level
of 15 µg/m³.
