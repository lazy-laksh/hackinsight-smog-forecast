## Who is sensitive
The guide names the sensitive groups for particle pollution as people with heart or lung disease, older adults
(who may have undiagnosed heart or lung disease) and children, whose lungs are still developing.

## Recommended actions by AQI category
Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups: people with heart or lung disease, older adults and children should reduce prolonged
or heavy exertion.
Unhealthy: people with heart or lung disease, older adults and children should avoid prolonged or heavy exertion;
everyone else should reduce prolonged or heavy exertion.
Very Unhealthy: people with heart or lung disease, older adults and children should avoid all physical activity
outdoors; everyone else should avoid prolonged or heavy exertion.
Note: the guide is from 2003, so its concentration ranges are older than the 2024 breakpoints; only its health
advice is used here.
